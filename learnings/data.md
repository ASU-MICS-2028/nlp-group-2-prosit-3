## What was done

In the Hugging Face viewer, each row pairs a recording with a reference transcript. The `accent` and `domain` columns let us identify clips for a specific accent and speaking context. Elise played the proposed clinical Setswana clip and found its reference transcript.

## How to check it

Open the viewer, select `dev`, and find the clinical Setswana row whose transcript starts with “rickets in growing children.” Play its audio and read the `transcript` column.

## Glossary

- Reference transcript — the dataset's written text for a recording, used to check the model's output.
- Pilot clip — one recording used to check that the workflow runs.
